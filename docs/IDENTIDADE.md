# Símbolo Central Usinagem

Criado com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens, a partir do pedido de duas engrenagens minimalistas em 3D. O texto da marca permanece editável em HTML.

- Original transparente: `design/brand-gears-original.png` (1254 × 1254 px, RGBA).
- Versão de interface: `public/images/brand-gears.png` (160 × 160 px, PNG transparente, aproximadamente 32 KB).
- Uso: cabeçalho e favicon. A redução para a interface preserva a imagem original e seu canal alfa.

## Prompt final

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: compact website brand icon for Central Usinagem, an industrial machining company. Create exactly two meshing minimal gears, one slightly larger brushed silver steel gear at upper left and one smaller electric-blue anodized metal gear at lower right. Premium restrained 3D product rendering, shallow machined depth and subtle bevels, front view with very slight perspective. Bold simple silhouette with 8 broad teeth per gear and simple circular holes; no intricate tiny details. Square composition, icon nearly fills canvas with 6% safe padding. Actual transparent background. No text, no letters, no additional objects, no floor, no backdrop, no cast shadow outside silhouette. Clean readable at 40px on a dark navy website.
```
